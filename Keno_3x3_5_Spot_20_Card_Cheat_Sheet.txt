KENO 3x3 / 5-SPOT / 20-CARD COVERAGE WHEEL

Purpose
Use one cramped 3x3 block on the Keno board. Each of the 20 cards uses 5 spots from that same 3x3 block.
The goal is not to predict the draw. The goal is to arrange the 20 cards so that if the draw gets lucky inside
your 3x3 block, the same cluster can pay on as many cards as possible.

Important
Keno is still random. This sheet does not make the 3x3 block more likely to hit.
It only gives you a stronger 20-card layout after numbers do land inside that block.


POSITION TEMPLATE

Name the 3x3 block like this:

1  2  3
4  5  6
7  8  9

If your 3x3 block is:

65 66 67

3 = 57

WHY THIS LAYOUT

Inside a 3x3 box there are 126 possible 5-number groups.
With only 20 cards, the most exact 5-of-5 groups you can cover is 20.

This wheel does that, and it also spreads the cards so that bigger clusters have fewer dead shapes:

If exactly 5 numbers land in your 3x3 box:
- 20 of the 126 possible 5-number clusters are exact 5-of-5 cards.
- Many of the other 5-number clusters still create several 4-of-5 cards.

If exactly 6 numbers land in your 3x3 box:
- 75 of the 84 possible 6-number clusters produce at least one 5-of-5 card.
- 5 of those clusters produce two 5-of-5 cards.
- Every 6-number cluster produces at least five 4-of-5 cards.

If exactly 7 numbers land in your 3x3 box:
- Every possible 7-number cluster produces either three or four 5-of-5 cards.
- Every possible 7-number cluster also produces ten to twelve 4-of-5 cards.

If exactly 8 numbers land in your 3x3 box:
- Every possible 8-number cluster produces eight to ten 5-of-5 cards.

If all 9 numbers land in your 3x3 box:
- All 20 cards are 5-of-5.

HOW TO USE WITH ANY OTHER 3x3 BLOCK

1. Pick a 3x3 block on the 1-80 board.
2. Label the block positions 1 through 9, left to right, top to bottom.
3. Put the five positions listed for Card A on card A.
4. Repeat for Cards B through T.

Example:
If position 1 is 24, position 2 is 25, position 3 is 26,
position 4 is 34, position 5 is 35, position 6 is 36,
position 7 is 44, position 8 is 45, and position 9 is 46,
then Card A is 24,25,26,34,45.
